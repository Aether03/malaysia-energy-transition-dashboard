I am a recent Electrical Engineering graduate of Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), and I am building a non-commercial data-analytics portfolio project on Malaysia's electricity transition. I am writing to request TNB's prior written consent, as required by the Terms & Conditions of www.gso.org.my, to use data published on the "System Demand Profile" page.

**Data requested**
Peninsular Malaysia system demand (MW, 10-minute intervals) as published on https://www.gso.org.my/SystemData/SystemDemand.aspx, for the period January 2017 to the present.

**Intended use**
- Download the historical series once, then refresh it at most monthly, with low-rate requests only.
- Store it privately on my own computer and aggregate it to hourly values.
- Analyse typical daily load profiles, monthly peak demand and load factor, and build a day-ahead demand forecast model for learning purposes.
- Publish only **aggregated charts and derived statistics** (for example, average daily load curves, monthly peaks, forecast accuracy) in a public GitHub repository and on my personal portfolio website, with the attribution "Source: Grid System Operator (GSO), Tenaga Nasional Berhad".

**What I will not do**
- Redistribute the raw 10-minute data in any form.
- Use the data or outputs for any commercial purpose.
- Suggest any endorsement by GSO or TNB.

I would also be grateful for permission to cite and link to the GSO website as the data source, since the Terms & Conditions ask for consent before linking.

I am happy to follow any conditions you set, such as specific attribution wording, a minimum aggregation level, or limits on how often the data is accessed, and I can share the finished work for your review before it is published. Until I receive your written consent, the project uses only openly licensed data, and no GSO data is stored or published.
